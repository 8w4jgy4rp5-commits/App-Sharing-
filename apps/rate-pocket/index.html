How to use

  1. Pick your home currency on the left and the local one on the right.
  2. Type the number written on the price tag. The big figure underneath is what it costs you.
  3. Rates are saved on this phone, so the app still answers on the plane or with no signal. Tap Refresh when you are back online.

Market rates, for orientation only. Banks, cards and exchange counters add their own fee, so the amount you are actually charged will differ.
