      How to use

      1. 1
        Copy your homepage. Open your own site, select all the text and paste it into the box. Add your pricing and FAQ text underneath if you have those pages.
      2. 2
        Run the check. You get a score out of 100 and ten rows. Each row is something an assistant needs before it can name you in an answer — and, where it is missing, the sentence to add.
      3. 3
        Work the channel list top-down. It is ordered for your product type and your gaps. Do the first one this week, not all five at once.
      4. 4
        Save it and come back. Next month, paste your updated page and run it again — the score shows the change since your last saved check for the same product.

      Saved checks stay in this browser. They are not shared with anyone else.
