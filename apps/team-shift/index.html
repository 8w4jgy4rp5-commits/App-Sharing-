    How to use

    1. Add each person once under Team. The role is optional — it just helps you tell two people apart.
    2. Pick a day in the strip at the top, then press a person's shift button to set Morning, Day, Night or Off. You can change the start and end times before saving.
    3. Switch the top card to Month to see the whole month at once. Each square carries a coloured dot per shift — green Morning, blue Day, purple Night — and the number of people working. Tap a square to open that day below.
    4. The small number under each date is how many people are working that day. It turns red when nobody is covering the day, so gaps are hard to miss.
    5. Press Copy this week for the group chat to turn the whole week into plain text you can paste into your chat.

    The roster is saved in your browser only (and follows you to your own devices if you are signed in to CobbleWorks). Your team members do not see it until you send them the copied text.
