    How to play

    🌱→ 🌿→ 🐰→ 🦊
    1. Tap an empty tile to plant a 🌱 seedling. That is your only move!
    2. Seedlings grow into 🌿 grass. Grass withers after a while, so keep planting.
    3. When there is enough grass, 🐰 rabbits appear by themselves and eat the grass.
    4. When there are enough rabbits, 🦊 foxes appear and hunt the rabbits.
    5. Animals starve if they can't find food — where and when you plant controls the whole ecosystem.
    6. Meet the goal conditions and hold them for the required time to clear the stage.

    Your stage progress is saved in this browser (and synced when you are logged in).
