        How to play

        🌱→ 🌿→ 🐰→ 🦊
        1. Tap an empty tile to plant a 🌱 seedling. That is your only move!
        2. Seedlings grow into 🌿 grass. Grass withers after a while, so keep planting.
        3. The nearest 🐰 rabbit notices a new seedling the moment you plant it — a "!" pops over its head, and it hops there and waits for the seedling to grow. That is how you steer a rabbit.
        4. Grass spreads on its own into empty tiles beside it — so planting less can be the better move.
        5. When there is enough grass, 🐰 rabbits appear by themselves and eat the grass.
        6. When there are enough rabbits, 🦊 foxes appear and hunt the rabbits.
        7. A red line means a fox has locked on to a rabbit. A running rabbit is faster than a fox, but it needs somewhere to run — plant away from the fox and the rabbit will bolt for it.
        8. Rabbits rest after a meal, and a resting rabbit cannot run. That is when foxes get them.
        9. The arc under each animal is how full it is: green when fed, red when close to starving. Plant near the red ones.
        10. Worn brown ground means that patch has been grazed bare — fresh grass does better elsewhere.
        11. Animals starve if they can't find food — where and when you plant controls the whole ecosystem.
        12. Meet the goal conditions and hold them for the required time to clear the stage.
