    How to use

    1. Name the event, then add everything it needs — one thing at a time.
    2. Press I'll bring it on a line, type your name, and save. The line turns green and shows who has it.
    3. Changed your mind? Press Free it up and the line goes back to open. To delete a line for good, press the × on its right twice.
    4. Press Copy the list for the group chat and paste it into your chat so everyone can see what is still open.

    This list is saved in your browser. It is not automatically shared with other people — that is what the copy button is for. If you are signed in to CobbleWorks, the list follows you to your own other devices.
