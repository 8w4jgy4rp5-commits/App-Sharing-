    How to use

    1. Add the two or three things you actually want to change. A short list is easier to face later.
    2. Drag each bar to roughly how far along you feel. Rough is fine — this is a feeling, not a spreadsheet.
    3. When a new month starts, a check-in banner appears at the top. Press it, walk through the list, then finish.
    4. Open "Check-in history" on any resolution to see how it moved month by month.
