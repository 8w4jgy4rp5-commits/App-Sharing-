    How to use

    1. Type the title of a movie or show and press Add to watchlist.
    2. Use Where you found it to remember the source — a streaming service, a friend, a post you saw.
    3. Tap the round tick on the left when you have watched it. Tap it again to move it back.
    4. Use the tabs to see only what you still want to watch, and the search box to find a title.

    Your list is saved in this browser. Sign in on CobbleWorks to keep it on your other devices too.
