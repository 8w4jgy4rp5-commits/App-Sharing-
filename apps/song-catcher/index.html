Song Catcher

Jot down a song before you lose the moment. Look it up later.

How to use

  1. Catch it

    Type whatever you remember, even a fuzzy guess, and hit "Catch it".

  2. Look it up later

    When you have time, tap "Search" on a memo to look it up on Google.

  3. Check it off

    Found the song? Check the box to mark it done — it stays in your list, just dimmed.
