        How Book Snap works

        1. Create a tag

        Tags are the only way to browse — group books by topic, class, or however you like.

        2. Tap "+ Add book" and snap a photo

        Right after the photo, fill in the title, author, and tag(s), then save.

        3. Keep snapping more pages

        On the book's page list, tap the camera button anytime. Write a note if you like, then save.

        4. Read it back anytime

        Tap a tag, then a book, then a page thumbnail to see the photo and your note.
