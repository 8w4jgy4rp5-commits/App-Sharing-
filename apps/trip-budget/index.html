      How to use

      1. Name the trip and pick the currency you will spend in.
      2. Add a category for each thing you spend on — food, lodging, transport — with a limit for each.
      3. Log every expense as you pay it. The bar for that category fills up.
      4. Check the bar before you buy. A red bar means you have already passed the limit.
      5. Everything stays in this browser. Sign in on CobbleWorks to carry it to your other devices.
