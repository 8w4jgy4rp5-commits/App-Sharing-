    How to use

    • Read the chapters in order. Each one is a couple of screens long.
    • Answer the quick check at the end of a chapter. A wrong answer explains itself, so getting it wrong is useful.
    • A chapter is marked read once you finish its quick check. Your place is kept, so you can stop and come back.
    • Use the Glossary any time a word is unfamiliar.
    • Chapter 6 puts the whole cafe together. That is the one to re-read before you open a real report.
