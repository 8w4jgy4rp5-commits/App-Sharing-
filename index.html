    Before you ask

    The four questions everyone has.

    Is it actually free?
    Yes. There is no paid tier, no trial, and no plan to add one.
    Do I need an account?
    Not to browse, and not to use any of the apps. Posting a request, rating an app, leaving a comment or claiming a build signs you in with Google — an anonymous board fills up with abuse, and a name next to a post stops that.
    Where does my data go?
    Into your own browser. Nothing leaves your device unless you sign in and ask for it on a second device.
    Can I build one?
    Yes — that is the point. Take a request off the board, ship a small app, put it on the shelf.

    Two ways in

    It starts with something small that annoys you.

    Whichever side of it you are on, the first move is the same.

    Got a problem? Write it in one sentence and leave it on the board. Post a problem
    Got a free evening? Take a request off the board and build it. See open requests

    Both take a Google sign-in. Nothing else is asked of you.
