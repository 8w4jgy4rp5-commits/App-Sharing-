# X Article Writer

Source: @XCreators, "The ultimate guide to Articles on X" (2026-01-16). Use these rules as the
checklist when drafting or reviewing an X Article. Write the Article in the language the user asks
for (usually Japanese); talk to the user in Japanese per beginner-guide.

## Workflow

1. **Ask / confirm the purpose first** (skip if already clear):
   - What should the reader think, feel, or do after reading?
   - Who exactly is it for?
2. Propose 2–3 **title + first-sentence hook** options.
3. Write the draft following "Writing rules" below.
4. Self-edit with "Editing pass", then hand over the draft.
5. Finish with the "Pre-publish checklist" and suggest promotion steps.

## Writing rules

1. **Clear purpose** — one purpose per Article; every section must serve it.
2. **Title & hook** — specific, sparks curiosity, promises value. Pair with a strong first sentence.
   - Bad: "Tips for Better Productivity in 2026"
   - Good: "Why 95% of Productivity Advice Fails"
   - Suggest a **header image** that is eye-catching and on-topic.
3. **Skimmable structure** (most readers are on mobile):
   - Paragraphs of 2–4 lines max, one idea per paragraph
   - A subheading every 3–5 paragraphs
   - Bullets / numbered lists over walls of text
   - **Bold the key insight** in almost every section
4. **Natural, consistent voice** — talk like to a good friend, not a lecture hall. Use "you/your"
   (Japanese: 「あなた」「〜しよう」など語りかけ). Too "professional" = boring.
   - Bad: "Successful people always prioritize their tasks."
   - Good: "You need to stop doing everything — focus on the 3 things that actually move the needle."
5. **Show, don't just tell** — follow every claim immediately with evidence: a stat, a personal
   story, a before/after, or an embedded X post/Article.
6. **Visuals & formatting** — images, screenshots, charts, embedded posts; bold headers and spacing.
7. **Strong close** — don't fade out. Do one or more of:
   - Summarize the key points
   - Ask a question to spark replies
   - Ask readers to share or try one tip right now

## Editing pass

The first draft is for the writer; the second and third are for the reader.
- Cut **20–30%** of the word count.
- Remove filler: "very", "really", "in order to" (Japanese: 「とても」「本当に」「〜することができる」「〜ということ」など).
- Read it aloud (mentally) — rewrite any awkward sentence.

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Purpose and target reader are clear
- [ ] Title is specific + curious + promises value; first sentence hooks
- [ ] Header image chosen
- [ ] Short paragraphs, subheadings every 3–5 paragraphs, lists, bolded insights
- [ ] Every claim has evidence or an example
- [ ] Trimmed 20–30%, filler removed
- [ ] Ends with summary / question / call to action

## Growing the audience (suggest after the draft)

- **Tease before publishing** — a short teaser post or question a few hours before.
- **Pin the new Article** to the profile for the first 24–72 hours.
- **Break it into a thread** — key excerpts as posts linking back to the Article.
- **Reply to early readers** — first-wave replies boost visibility.
- **Reshare older Articles** when they become relevant to the news again (evergreen content).

## Monetizing with Subscriptions (only if the user wants it)

- Clearly state subscriber value: deep dives, early access, exclusive opinions, behind-the-scenes, series.
- Soft paywall: keep some Articles free to attract readers; reserve depth for subscribers.
- Subscriber-only formats: weekly memo, industry brief, portfolio updates, Q&A, private breakdowns.
- Thank subscribers publicly now and then (social proof).
- End with a short "Next week:" preview to build habit.
- How to publish: Publish → "Choose your audience" → **Subscribers** → Publish.
- Promote to non-subscribers with a teaser post linking to
  `https://x.com/YOURUSERNAME/creator-subscriptions/subscribe`.
- Eligibility: check in Creator Studio. (Articles are available to all Premium subscribers.)
